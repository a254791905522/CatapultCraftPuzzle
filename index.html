Catapult Craft Puzzle is a casual merge-and-launch puzzle game where you spawn numbered blocks, merge matching pieces to upgrade them, and fire your creation at 3D targets to complete each build. How to play

  1. Spawn and merge — Tap to drop numbered objects on the board; match identical numbers to upgrade tiers.
  2. Aim and launch — Load your upgraded piece into the catapult and fire at the 3D target to snap it into place.
  3. Beat the clock — Finish each objective before your moves or time run out.
  4. Explore craft worlds — Unlock new themes, sharper builds, and rescue power-ups when you need a second chance.
